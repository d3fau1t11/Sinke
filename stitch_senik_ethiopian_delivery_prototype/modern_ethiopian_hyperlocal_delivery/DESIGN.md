## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the warmth, pace, and communal spirit of urban Addis Ababa. It rejects generic Western tech minimalism in favor of an identity steeped in Ethiopian hospitality (*mar* and *berbere* tones, the communal generosity of *senik*—food prepared for journeys) while maintaining high-velocity Android utility.

The personality balances three pillars:
1. **Grounded Craft:** Subtle visual cadence derived from traditional *tibeb* textile geometry—restrained, precise, and applied as delicate edge borders or micro-separators rather than decorative clutter.
2. **Hyperlocal Utilitarianism:** Tuned explicitly for suburban Addis expansion zones (Tuludimtu, Koye Feche, Akaki Kality) where mobile network variance, bright high-altitude daylight, and localized landmark addresses demand high contrast, legible hierarchy, and immediate feedback.
3. **Warm Modernity:** Material 3 foundation upgraded with warm, baked earth tones, deep roasted coffee neutrals, and vibrant fresh acacia greens. The tone is respectful, reassuring, brisk, and community-centric.

## Colors

The palette grounds modern digital transactions in the sensory world of Ethiopian culinary craft and botanical resilience:

- **Primary (`#D9531E` - Awaze Terracotta):** Represents vitality, appetite, and purposeful motion. Used for critical forward actions, active status indicators, and main interactive touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#2E7D32` - Acacia Grove Green):** The color of harvest, fresh produce, verified vendor badges, on-schedule delivery tracking, and positive balances.
- **Tertiary (`#E58E26` - Honey Amber / Ochre):** Celebratory and highlighting. Used for priority announcements, merchant ratings, promotional tags, and express checkout accents.
- **Neutral (`#2D1F17` - Roasted Buna):** A rich, ultra-deep roasted brown replacing harsh digital black. It delivers crisp text contrast without cold sterile undertones.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FDFBF7` to `#F7F3EC` - Teff Cream):** Off-white base surfaces that soften screen glare under direct equatorial sun and imbue surfaces with an organic warmth.
- **State Colors:** Muted chili red (`#BA1A1A`) for cancellation and errors; pale eucalyptus (`#E8F5E9`) for successful milestone chips.

## Typography

Typography relies on a coordinated dual-script strategy. For Latin characters, **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides open, friendly, geometric confidence in titles, paired with **Inter** for dense transactional lists, order receipts, and micro-labels. 

For the Ethiopic script (Fidel / ግዕዝ), the system pairs with **Noto Sans Ethiopic**, matched optically to the weight and x-height of Inter and Plus Jakarta Sans:
- **Optical Baseline Alignment:** Ethiopic characters feature distinct vertical stems and vowel diacritics. Line heights are expanded by an additional 12% across body and label tiers to prevent vowel mark clipping.
- **Weight Calibration:** Bold Ethiopic text uses weight 600 instead of 700 to ensure intricate glyphs (e.g., ቋ, ጯ, ጵ) do not fill in or blur on low-DPI mobile screens.
- **Numeral Pairing:** Telebirr, CBE Birr, and cash values display numerals in bold tabular Inter figures (`tnum`), followed by the currency mark (`ETB` or `ብር`) scaled down to 80% baseline offset.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for fluid touch ergonomics on handheld devices. A 4-column layout on mobile scales to 8 columns on tablet devices:

- **Thumb Zone Focus:** Primary conversion targets (cart controls, checkout sheets, live rider call buttons) sit anchored within the bottom 35% of the viewport.
- **Hyperlocal Address Grids:** Card layouts prioritize stacked geographic data (Site / Condominium Block / Gate / Landmark) over horizontal truncations, accommodating long neighborhood designations like "Koye Feche Site 2, Block 42".
- **Dense Data Accommodation:** Product item lists utilize a compact vertical cadence (`space-sm` for list gutters, `space-md` for row padding) to allow dense multi-item grocery and meal browsing without excessive scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

This system eschews generic grey box shadows in favor of tinted, warm ambient depth inspired by clay and roasted grounds:

1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background sits at `#FDFBF7`. Flat and matte.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Uses `#FFFFFF` surface fills with a dual outline-and-diffuse technique:
   - A subtle 1px border colored with `#EFE8DD` (Teff shell).
   - An ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(45, 31, 23, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Drag / Modals / Sticky Navigation):** Raised above map and scroll views:
   - Elevated border: `1px solid rgba(217, 83, 30, 0.12)`.
   - Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 31, 23, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 31, 23, 0.04)`.
4. **Level 3 (Floating Action & Urgent Alerts):** Bottom checkout sheet and floating delivery updates:
   - Shadow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(45, 31, 23, 0.16)`.
5. **Tibeb Geometry Edge Treatment:** Selected elevated hero cards and header banners incorporate a top 2px rhythmic micro-striping using alternating terracotta, gold ochre, and deep buna diamonds, providing tactile distinction without visual noise.

## Shapes

The design uses a Level 2 (Rounded) standard to harmonize with modern Android gesture ergonomics:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Chips):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radius. This keeps compact elements crisp and orderly in inventory arrays.
- **Containers & Cards:** `1rem` (`16px`) border radius. Cards look welcoming, modern, and easily distinguishable against the cream canvas.
- **Bottom Sheets & Modal Overlays:** `1.5rem` (`24px`) top-corner radius, delivering a clean sheet peel effect for location pickers and payment option sheets.
- **Touch Pill Targets:** Cart counters, vehicle type selectors (Bajaj, Motorbike, Carry van), and "Order Now" CTAs use fully rounded pill structures (`9999px`) to maximize hit confidence.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Delivery Confirmation / Add to Cart):** Background `#D9531E`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600. Minimum height 52px to ensure one-handed ease of tapping while walking. Active state depresses subtly with a scale down to `0.98`.
- **Secondary (Direct Driver Call / View Details):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #2D1F17`, text `#2D1F17`.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Unframed `#D9531E` or `#2E7D32` with a weight of 600 and a padded touch frame of 48px.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Small, high-contrast pills. Fresh / Open (`#E8F5E9` background, `#2E7D32` text); Busy / Surge (`#FFF3E0` background, `#D9531E` text).
- **Cuisine & Category Filter Chips:** Horizontal scrolling pills with `#FFFFFF` background, `#2D1F17` text, and a fine `#EFE8DD` outline. Selected state toggles to `#2D1F17` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Inputs & Hyperlocal Selectors
- **Address & Search Fields:** 52px height, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E0D7CB` border, 8px radius. Placeholder text in `#7A6F68`. Focused state reveals a 2px `#D9531E` ring with zero exterior spread.
- **Landmark Helper Input:** Integrated icon prefix showing a neighborhood beacon/flag, paired with secondary helper chips (e.g., "Near Total Station", "Block Gate 2", "Behind Condominium Market").

### Cards
- **Merchant & Restaurant Cards:** Top media card with 16:9 ratio, featuring a top-right delivery speed badge (e.g., "15–25 min • ባጃጅ"). Bottom body includes store title in `title-lg`, rating with a tertiary gold ochre star (`#E58E26`), and neighborhood tier subtitle.
- **Order Tracking Progress Card:** White container featuring vertical step indicators styled as clean geometric beads. Active transit stage pulses with an acacia green ring (`#2E7D32`).

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Toggles
- **Toggles (Online/Offline, Utensils):** Custom track colored `#E3DCD3` in inactive state; turns `#2E7D32` when active. Thumb is always crisp white with Level 1 elevation.
- **Payment Method Selectors (Telebirr, CBE, Cash on Delivery):** Radio cards framed with 1.5px border that shifts from neutral `#EFE8DD` to `#D9531E` upon selection, with the brand logo prominent on the left.

### Custom Component: Delivery Vehicle / Mode Switcher
Segmented pill container displaying local transit options (Bajaj / 3-wheeler, Motorbike, Small Van). Each option showcases a minimal two-tone icon, estimated arrival window, and local road-capacity tag.
